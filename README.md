# 🛍️ E-Commerce Sales Analysis

This project showcases an **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** of a real-world e-commerce dataset to derive business insights related to sales performance, product profitability, customer segments, and seasonal trends. It is designed to demonstrate practical skills in data cleaning, visualization, and business storytelling.

---

## 📦 Dataset Overview

- **Key Columns**:
  - `Order Date`, `Ship Date`
  - `Sales`, `Profit`, `Quantity`
  - `Category`, `Sub-Category`
  - `Customer Segment`, `Region`, `State`

---

## ❓ Business Questions Explored

1. 📈 What is the **monthly sales trend**? Which month has the highest and lowest sales?
2. 🛒 Which **product category** has the highest and lowest sales?
3. 🔍 What are the **sales figures across sub-categories**?
4. 💹 What is the **monthly profit trend**, and which month had the highest profit?
5. 🧮 What is the **profit by category and sub-category**?
6. 👥 How do **customer segments** differ in terms of sales and profit?
7. 📊 What is the **sales-to-profit ratio**, and how does it vary?

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- 🏆 **November** had the **highest sales**, while **February** saw the lowest.
- 🖥️ **Technology** was the top-selling category, while **Office Supplies** had the least sales.
- 💰 Monthly profits peaked in **December**, showing strong seasonal trends.
- ❗ **Copiers** underperformed in profit and may need re-evaluation.
- 👤 The **Consumer** segment is the most profitable.
- 💹 The average **sales-to-profit ratio** was **8.66%**, varying widely across categories.
